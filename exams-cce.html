Examination & CCE structure and guidelines

Examination Structure

  • Unit Tests: Three unit tests conducted during the academic session (30 marks each)
  • Half-Yearly Examination: Conducted in the month of July (80 marks)
  • Pre-Board Examination: Conducted in the month of December (80 marks)
  • Annual / Final Examination: Conducted in February – March (80 marks)
  • Internal Assessment: Carried out throughout the academic year as per CBSE norms

Continuous and Comprehensive Evaluation (CCE)

As per CBSE guidelines, CCE aims to evaluate the overall development of students through continuous assessment.

  • Formative Assessments: Regular class tests, quizzes, assignments, projects, and group discussions
  • Summative Assessments: Term-end examinations
  • Co-Scholastic Areas: Art Education, Health and Physical Education, Work Experience, and Life Skills
  • Grading System: Grades A1 to E are awarded based on performance
  • Report Cards: Issued after each term, reflecting both scholastic and co-scholastic progress

Reference: As per CBSE Circular No. 39/2018 and subsequent guidelines

Examination Guidelines (Code of Conduct)

  • Punctuality: Students must report to the examination hall 15 minutes before the scheduled time
  • Admit Card: Carrying the admit card is mandatory for all examinations
  • Dress Code: Full school uniform is compulsory on all examination days
  • Prohibited Items: Mobile phones, smartwatches, calculators, and other electronic devices are strictly prohibited
  • Re-evaluation: Applications for re-evaluation can be submitted within 7 days of result declaration as per CBSE norms
