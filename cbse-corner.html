About CBSE

The Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE) is a national-level board of education in India, established in 1962. It is managed and controlled by the Government of India and is responsible for conducting examinations for Class 10 and Class 12, and prescribing curriculum for affiliated schools.

Swami Vivekanand Govt. Model School, Bhim, is affiliated with CBSE and strictly adheres to the curriculum, assessment patterns, and guidelines prescribed by the Board.

Curriculum and Syllabus

The CBSE curriculum is designed to promote intellectual, social, and emotional development of students. It emphasizes:

  • Scholastic Areas: Languages, Mathematics, Science, Social Science, and Elective Subjects as per CBSE norms
  • Co-Scholastic Areas: Art Education, Health and Physical Education, Work Experience, and Life Skills
  • Assessment: Continuous and Comprehensive Evaluation (CCE) as per CBSE guidelines
  • Textbooks: NCERT textbooks are prescribed for all subjects and classes

Board Examinations (Class 10 and 12)

  • Theory Examinations: Conducted annually as per CBSE schedule (February – March)
  • Practical Examinations: Conducted in January – February as per CBSE guidelines
  • Admit Card: Issued by the school as per CBSE instructions
  • Result Declaration: As per CBSE official announcement (usually in May)
  • Re-evaluation and Compartment: Processed as per CBSE norms
