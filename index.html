About School — a legacy of excellence

Swami Vivekanand Govt. Model School, Bhim

Our institution is a beacon of academic excellence and holistic development. Affiliated with the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE), we are committed to nurturing young minds through a blend of modern pedagogy and timeless values.

Our school is named after the great philosopher Swami Vivekanand, whose teachings inspire us to build character, foster courage, and instil a spirit of service.

📍 Vision & Mission

Vision: To create a vibrant learning community where every student discovers their potential.

Mission: To provide a safe, inclusive, and stimulating environment that encourages curiosity, critical thinking, and collaboration.

📚 Academic Approach

We follow a learner-centric curriculum that integrates experiential learning, project-based assignments, and continuous assessment. The school offers Science, Commerce, and Humanities streams at the senior secondary level.
